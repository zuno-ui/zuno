# Code of Conduct

ZUNO welcomes people of different backgrounds, identities and experience levels.
Participation should be respectful and free from harassment in issues, pull
requests, discussions and other spaces operated by the project. These standards
also apply when someone officially represents ZUNO elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the code and evidence rather than attacking a person.
- Give actionable feedback and allow others room to learn or disagree.
- Respect boundaries, privacy and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help repair their effects.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation and retaliation against a reporter are not acceptable. Technical
disagreement and respectful criticism are welcome.

## Reporting

For project moderation, email [cristian.mendez@hemia.mx](mailto:cristian.mendez@hemia.mx)
with links, relevant context and your preferred way to be contacted. Share only
the information needed to investigate; do not post incident details publicly.

For abuse occurring on GitHub, you can also use its private
[Report abuse process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Those reports go to GitHub, not automatically to ZUNO maintainers.

Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Reports are handled as confidentially as possible.
